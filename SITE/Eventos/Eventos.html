Join Vendedoors Events!

At Vendedoor’s, selling goes far beyond offering a product. It's about stories, exchange of experiences, and growing together as a community. Each sale is an opportunity to connect, share knowledge, and strengthen real relationships between sellers and customers.

That’s why we promote and support events that bring together sellers, customers, and delivery partners in unique experiences. Our fairs, workshops, and meetings are spaces where door-to-door sellers showcase their talents live, share knowledge, and create bonds with those who value handmade, artisanal, and local work.

Whether it’s a fair in your neighborhood square, an online crochet workshop, or a meeting to exchange ideas among sellers, here you’ll always find a way to get involved.
