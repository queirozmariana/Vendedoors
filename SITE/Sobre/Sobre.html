Who We Are

Vendedoor’s was born with a simple purpose: to strengthen the work of door-to-door sellers in the digital world. We know how this form of sales is direct, human, and personal and we believe it deserves more visibility, autonomy, and support. We created a platform that connects local sellers with people interested in their products, in a practical and safe way.


Vision

Our vision is to become the leading digital platform dedicated to door-to-door sellers, offering not only visibility but also recognition and appreciation for these professionals. We aim to bring people closer and create a space where local businesses can have the same relevance as big companies in the digital environment.



Mission

At Vendedoor’s, our mission is to support door-to-door micro-sellers, especially women resellers, elderly entrepreneurs, and families seeking extra income. We’ve built a simple, accessible, and secure platform designed for those who are often forgotten by the digital market.
